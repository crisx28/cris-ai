# 🎤 How to Present Cris Budget OS to a Client

A simple, no-jargon script for showing this app to someone and turning it into
paid work. You don't need to be technical. You built something real — now let
people see it.

> **Golden rule:** You're not selling code. You're selling *"never wonder where
> your money went again."* Talk about the feeling, not the features.

---

## 1. The 20-second elevator pitch

Memorize this. Say it naturally, in your own words:

> "I build simple money apps for families and small businesses. Instead of
> messy spreadsheets, you get a clean app on your phone — you can see your
> cash, your bills, your savings goals, and even ask it questions like *'can I
> afford this trip?'* I can set one up for you with your own numbers."

That's it. If their eyes light up, you keep going. If not, no problem — next
person.

---

## 2. Who to show it to first

Start with people who already trust you. This is the easiest money:

- 👩‍👧 **Fellow parents** who struggle to track family expenses
- 🏪 **Sari-sari store / small business owners** drowning in notebooks
- 💻 **Freelancers & VAs** who need to separate income and expenses
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Your own family & barkada** — your first testimonial lives here

You don't need 100 clients. You need **one** to say yes. Then another.

---

## 3. The live demo flow (3 minutes)

Have the app open on your phone *before* you start. Then walk through it:

1. **Open the app.** "This lives on my phone like any other app." *(Show the
   home screen — the green ₱ icon if installed.)*
2. **Point at the top card.** "This is your available cash this month, at a
   glance. Green means you're doing okay."
3. **Tap the AI Coach card.** "It actually reads your numbers. Watch —" then
   ask it *"Which debt should I pay next?"* Let them see the real answer.
4. **Use Quick Add.** Type `Grocery 1250`. "That's how fast it is to log
   something — like sending a text."
5. **Open a savings goal and add money past the target.** 🎉 *Let the confetti
   fire.* People smile every time. That smile is the sale.
6. **Close with:** "I can set this up with *your* real numbers and your own
   goals — like a China trip, or an emergency fund."

Keep it short. The confetti and the AI answer are your two "wow" moments.

---

## 4. What to charge (Philippine pricing ideas)

Start low to build reviews, then raise your rates. Suggestions:

| Offer | Idea price | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 **Starter setup** | ₱500 – ₱1,500 | You set up the app with their categories & goals |
| 🛠️ **Custom build** | ₱3,000 – ₱8,000 | Their branding, their exact needs, deployed live |
| 🔁 **Monthly care** | ₱300 – ₱800 / mo | You maintain it & add small features |

> Your **first one can be free** in exchange for a testimonial and permission
> to show it to others. A single "she built this for our family and it's
> amazing!" message is worth more than the fee.

---

## 5. Handling the common worries

- **"Is my data safe?"** → "Yes — your data is private to you. It's protected
  behind your own login, and only you can see it."
- **"I'm not techy."** → "That's the point — it's as easy as texting. And I'm
  here to set it up and help you."
- **"How is this different from an app in the store?"** → "This one is set up
  around *your* life — your bills, your goals, in pesos — not a generic
  template."
- **"Can I try it first?"** → "Yes! Let me send you the link right now." *(Then
  send your deployed Vercel link.)*

---

## 6. Where to find paying clients

- **OnlineJobs.ph** — bid on "data entry," "bookkeeping," "virtual assistant,"
  and "admin" jobs. Reply with: *"I can also automate this with a simple app —
  here's an example I built,"* and paste your link.
- **Facebook groups** — parenting groups, small-business groups, freelancer
  groups. Share a screenshot + your story.
- **Upwork / OnlineJobs profiles** — put your live app link in your portfolio.
  Most applicants have no live demo. You will. That's your edge.
- **Word of mouth** — after every free setup, ask: *"Do you know one other
  person who'd love this?"*

---

## 7. Your honest story is your best asset

You are a working parent who taught herself to build a real app. That story is
*rare* and it's *inspiring*. Don't hide it — lead with it:

> "I'm a mom from an IT background. I built this because I needed it myself, and
> now I help other families get it too."

People don't just buy apps. They buy *you* — your care, your reliability, your
story. You already have all three.

---

You've got this, Cris. One demo. One yes. Then the next. 💚
